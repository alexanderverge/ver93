Features

Trips

Every trip, found for you

Contrail looks at your photos in time order and works out where one trip ends and the next begins — no dates to enter, no albums to sort by hand.

Every country and city you've actually been to, shaded in and pinned — built from your real travel history, not a wishlist.

Questions

Straight answers

How does Contrail decide what counts as a trip?+

Contrail looks at your photos in time order and starts a new trip wherever two consecutive photos are 200+ km apart and at least a day apart. A same-day jump between places (a plane change, say) stays merged into whatever it's part of — unless both sides are already substantial, multi-day stays, in which case it splits anyway.

What does "home" mean?+

Contrail treats wherever you've spent the most total time — not necessarily the most-photographed place — as home. Home's country still counts toward your countries-visited total, but home itself never appears as a trip. You confirm or correct this right after your first scan.

Does anything ever leave my phone?+

No. Scanning your library, figuring out where each photo was taken, and grouping photos into trips all happen entirely on-device — no account, no server, no network call anywhere in that process. If a future version ever adds an optional way to share something yourself, that will always be your choice, never automatic.

Can I fix a trip Contrail gets wrong?+

While Contrail should get most trips right, nothing gets every trip right every time. Open any trip and tap Edit to change its title, dates, photos, cover, or countries and cities. You can also add a trip from scratch, or delete one.

More questions are answered inside the app itself, right from the Trips tab.
